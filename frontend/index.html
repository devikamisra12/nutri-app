How much dietary leucine is needed per meal to maximally trigger muscle protein synthesis (MPS)?
JD
AI Nutrition Specialist 10:44 AM Response Validated
To saturate the "leucine trigger" mechanism that stimulates mTORC1 and drives maximal muscle protein synthesis, healthy adults typically require approximately 2.5 to 3.0 grams of leucine per feeding bolus. In whole-food equivalents, this translates to roughly 25–35 grams of high-quality intact protein such as whey isolate, eggs, Greek yogurt, or lean poultry.
Validated Claims & Clinical Insights (3)

A threshold dose of approximately 2.5–3.0g leucine is required to reach the maximal intracellular amino acid signalling threshold (mTORC1 activation) in young recreationally active individuals.

Evidence Source: Am J Clin Nutr / Norton & Layman (2006)

Older adults experience anabolic resistance, frequently necessitating higher single-meal doses (3.5–4.0g leucine or ~40g total protein) to achieve comparable myofibrillar protein synthesis rates.

Evidence Source: Wall et al., Clin Nutr (2015)

Plant-based proteins typically possess lower leucine concentrations by weight (6–8% vs 11–13% in whey), usually necessitating larger serving sizes or grain-legume complementation.
